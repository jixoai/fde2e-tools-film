# Credits — One Prompt (FDE2E edition)

This edition is adapted from `openfilm/examples/one-prompt` at
`65d717b7ba27b9e665dc6bb6e8e3b6579048288d`. The source project and its code,
film and song are distributed by the OpenFilm contributors under MIT.

## AI-generated music

`assets/one-prompt.mp3`, "One Prompt", was generated with ElevenLabs Music by the
OpenFilm contributors for the upstream film. `assets/data/audio.json` was computed from
that song. The file is explicitly identified as AI-generated in `EXAMPLE-METADATA.json`
and `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md`.

## Code

- The film engine in `src/px/` derives from pdoom-video © 2026 Giacomo Magnanini,
  MIT (`src/px/LICENSE-pdoom-video.txt`).
- three.js 0.184.0 is MIT and is self-hosted from `vendor/three/`; no runtime CDN is used.

## Fonts

Archivo, Archivo Black, Cormorant Garamond and IBM Plex Mono use SIL OFL 1.1. Hershey
Sans retains its own notice. Full texts and copyright notices are in
`assets/fonts/OFL.txt` and `assets/fonts/stroke/HersheySans1.svg`.

## Brand adaptation

The upstream OpenFilm mark and wordmark end plate was removed. The final plate in this
edition displays `FDE2E` and `FILM.TOOL.FDE2E.COM`; the original trademark geometry is
not distributed in this project.
